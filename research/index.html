Research

AJ Schwartz

PhD Student, Experimental Psychology (Psycholinguistics), UC San Diego

I study how people plan and time their speech for the person they’re talking to, and what happens when a medium like video calling gets in the way.

Research themes

  • 01

    Conversation over video calls

    How audio delay and a flattened visual channel affect the timing of turn-taking, and how speakers come across.

  • 02

    Judging speaker confidence

    How listeners hear confidence in the way someone talks, and whether reading a transcript changes that judgment.

  • 03

    Humans and LLMs as language judges

    How large language models compare to human raters when judging confidence in dialogue.
